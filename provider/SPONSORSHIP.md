# Antseed Directory — sponsorship plan

Antseed Directory is part of Antseed Markets. These are proposed commercial products, not existing bookings or verified audience/traffic claims.

## Positioning

Sell relevant exposure to people exploring providers, AI services and the surrounding ecosystem. Do not sell an implied investment recommendation or an official Antseed endorsement.

Keep basic provider pages and ordinary discovery useful without payment. Commercial placements should fund the directory without undermining its usefulness or neutrality.

## Initial inventory

### Featured provider

A distinct Sponsored spotlight linking to a provider profile. Start with a small number of fixed-duration placements, visually separate from ordinary results. Do not silently promote the sponsor within organic sorting or present it as the best provider.

### Category partner

A labelled sponsor panel on a relevant category, such as coding, research or private inference. One sponsor per category per agreed period is a proposed packaging option, not a current exclusivity promise. Category relevance and content approval come before payment.

### Directory / founding partner

A restrained site-level acknowledgement and partner introduction. Suitable for ecosystem infrastructure or complementary tools. Sell a defined pilot term and specified placements, not permanent ownership of directory real estate. This supports early development before there is enough traffic to justify impression-based pricing.

### Sponsored guide or interview

An educational tutorial, case study or provider interview with prominent sponsorship disclosure. Review accuracy and claims; do not grant sponsors a veto over unrelated directory facts or critical safety information. Published material needs a documented hosting term, update policy and end-of-campaign labelling policy.

## Opportunities for later

- A sponsored slot in a genuine opt-in newsletter, once a newsletter and audience actually exist.
- Co-hosted demonstrations or workshops with clear sponsor attribution.
- Paid provider tools such as richer analytics or additional presentation options, priced separately from advertising and identity verification.
- An agreed cross-site package spanning the directory and Antseed Markets, only after both sites' owners approve inventory and placement details.

None of these later features are implemented.

## Pilot pricing approach

Do not invent rate-card prices or promise audience numbers before measuring real usage and demand. Begin with a small, fixed-fee founding pilot, agreed manually, with:

- Placement and duration.
- Creative/content specification and review.
- Any exclusivity explicitly bounded by category and dates.
- Defined delivery/reporting and treatment of outages.
- Clear cancellation, refund, takedown and rejected-content terms.

A fixed placement fee is easier to explain initially than CPM/CPC when reliable inventory and measurement are not yet established. Revisit pricing using actual demand, audience fit and verified performance. Sponsorship does not guarantee leads, service purchases, token prices or returns.

## Measurement

A production implementation can report actual delivered impressions, profile visits and outbound clicks, with documented definitions and bot filtering. Do not call a click a customer, count internal preview traffic as an audience, or claim conversions at third-party destinations without proper attribution.

Decide privacy notice/consent and retention requirements before adding tracking. No tracking or ad analytics are currently installed.

## Trust and safety rules

- Clearly label paid exposure **Sponsored** at the placement itself.
- Keep verification, availability and objective network facts independent of payment.
- Distinguish provider-supplied descriptions from independently checked claims.
- Review outbound destinations and claims, especially privacy, financial and regulatory statements.
- No executable ad HTML/JavaScript, wallet approval flows or arbitrary embeds.
- Set complaint, moderation and emergency-removal procedures.
- Review advertising and financial-promotion rules for relevant target jurisdictions before accepting token-related campaigns.

## AntBeacon example campaign

AntBeacon is the selected example advertiser for this design pass and is shown as having taken all four proposed sponsorship options: Featured provider, Category partner, Directory/founding partner and Sponsored guide. This is a configured prototype campaign display, not a payment record, booking system or performance claim. Its website URL is intentionally not connected yet.

## Present prototype

The `#partners` page shows these formats, marks each as sponsored by AntBeacon and includes a clearly labelled placement mockup. Visitors can generate a local text-file brief containing their name, format, website and campaign goal. The form neither submits nor stores it. There is no checkout, booking backend, campaign schedule, paid ranking, newsletter or public enquiry inbox.

## Next implementation step

After Ellis approves the format and commercial terms, add a real, consented enquiry destination and a small reviewed-campaign administration flow. Store sponsorship separately from provider identity and organic directory data. Add scheduled start/end dates, approval status, disclosure text, destination validation and reporting before serving real paid placements.
